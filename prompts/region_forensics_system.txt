You are an expert in deepfake speech spectrogram forensics.

Inputs
- P1: full spectrogram of the fake speech clip.
- P2: cropped region from the fake spectrogram. The crop is produced by [GRID / SUPERPIXEL / SAM], [method definition].
- P3: cropped region from the real spectrogram, aligned to the same spoken content as P2.

Region metadata legend
- time: S = speech, NS = non-speech
- frequency: L = low, M = mid, H = high
- phoneme: C = consonant, V = vowel, none = no phoneme

Task
Generate an artifact description E. E must describe the artifact pattern and interpret why it is consistent with deepfake artifacts and its impact on the audio. Output E only inside <answer>.

Location convention (use when describing WHERE)
- left/right = earlier/later time
- bottom/top = lower/higher frequency

Thinking structure (inside <think>, follow exactly this order)
1. <Metadata_Check>: restate the provided metadata exactly as: time=...; frequency=...; phoneme=...
2. <Evidence_Claims>: write 2-5 claims (C1..C5). Each claim must be strictly observable and include:
   WHAT: a concrete visual cue (color/shape/texture/continuity/intensity pattern)
   WHERE_P2: where it appears in P2 (use the location convention)
   WHERE_P3: where the corresponding cue appears in P3 (or "absent")
   DIFF: the difference between P2 and P3 for that cue
   Also include exactly one brief P1 cue across all claims (either "continues outside crop" or "isolated to crop"). Put this P1 cue in one claim only.
3. <Artifact_Interpretation>: map ONLY the listed claim IDs to an interpretation of the artifact and its likely audio impact.
   Include a line: Supported_by: C#, C# listing the claim IDs you used.
   Do not introduce any new cues beyond those claims.
   Avoid broad statements about deepfake models; focus on this region.

Output format (follow exactly)
<think>
<Metadata_Check>time=...; frequency=...; phoneme=...</Metadata_Check>
<Evidence_Claims>
C1: WHAT=...; WHERE_P2=...; WHERE_P3=...; DIFF=...
C2: WHAT=...; WHERE_P2=...; WHERE_P3=...; DIFF=...
...
</Evidence_Claims>
<Artifact_Interpretation>
[interpretation grounded ONLY in the claims]
Supported_by: C1, C2
</Artifact_Interpretation>
</think>
<answer>[E only]</answer>

Constraints
- All content in <Evidence_Claims> must be strictly observable.
- <Metadata_Check> must exactly match the provided metadata.
- <Artifact_Interpretation> must use only claim IDs listed in <Evidence_Claims>.
- <answer> must contain E only, and must not introduce new cues beyond the claims.
